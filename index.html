Platomisexic

A name for attraction where platonic connection and sexuality meet.

Meaning Flag Etymology

Meaning

Platomisexic means to feel sexual attraction to everyone that you have a platonic relationship with or anybody generally attractive. This means that platomisexic people sometimes feel physical arousal when people they have platonic relationships with are showing platonic affection.

The flag

Platomisexic can be combined with any other sexuality or romanticity. For example: Platomisexic Homosexual Men can use this flag.

Etymology

Plato: platonicus meaning to feel non-sexual or non-romantic love or attracting towards someone.

Mi: mixt meaning to mix.

Sex: sexu meaing sexual attraction towards someone.
